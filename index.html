Privacy Policy for Our Daily Bread

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Our Daily Bread ("the App") is a simple daily Bible verse app. This policy explains what data the App does and does not collect.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics. It has no servers, no backend, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

The only information the App stores is kept entirely on your own device:

  • The date you first opened the App (used to determine which verse to show each day)
  • Your chosen daily reminder time
  • Whether daily reminders are turned on or off

None of this ever leaves your device. Uninstalling the App deletes it completely.

Notifications

The App uses your device's local notification system to deliver a daily verse reminder at the time you choose. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external server.

Third Parties

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version of the App adds a feature that involves data collection), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
